TechNova Solutions
Job Title: Junior Full Stack Developer Intern

About the Role
TechNova Solutions is looking for a Junior Full Stack Developer Intern to join our
product engineering team. You will work alongside senior engineers on customer-facing
web features, from the database up to the browser. This is a 6-month internship with a
strong possibility of conversion to a full-time role.

Responsibilities
- Build and maintain features across the front end and the back end of our web platform.
- Design and implement server-side endpoints that the web client consumes.
- Write queries and manage data models for our application database.
- Participate in code reviews and work with the team through our sprint cycle.
- Write tests for the code you ship and help debug production issues.

Requirements
- Solid understanding of JavaScript and modern front-end development.
- Experience building user interfaces with React.
- Hands-on experience with Node.js for server-side development.
- Must be comfortable designing and consuming REST APIs.
- Working knowledge of a database - we use MongoDB, but relational experience counts.
- Proficient with Git for version control and collaborative development.
- Strong grasp of HTML and CSS, including responsive layouts.
- Good problem solving skills and a solid grounding in data structures and algorithms.

Preferred Qualifications
- Familiarity with TypeScript is a plus.
- Exposure to Next.js or another React meta-framework would be an advantage.
- Basic understanding of Docker and containerised deployment is nice to have.
- Experience with unit testing frameworks such as Jest is desirable.
- Awareness of cloud platforms such as AWS is a bonus.

Eligibility
- Pursuing or recently completed a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science or a related field.
- CGPA of 7.5 or above.
- Candidates from tier-1 colleges preferred.
- Should be available to work from our Bengaluru office 3 days a week.

What We Offer
- Mentorship from senior full stack engineers.
- A monthly stipend and a pre-placement offer for strong performers.
