Job Title: Data Scientist Intern

About the Role
Work with our analytics team turning raw product data into models and decisions.

Responsibilities
- Clean, explore and analyse large datasets.
- Build and evaluate machine learning models for prediction and classification.
- Communicate findings through clear visualisations and written summaries.
- Write SQL to pull the data your analysis needs.

Requirements
- Strong Python skills, including Pandas and NumPy.
- Solid understanding of machine learning fundamentals and model evaluation.
- Must be proficient in SQL for data extraction and aggregation.
- Experience with scikit-learn or an equivalent modelling library.
- Grounding in statistics and experiment design.
- Comfortable with data visualization and presenting results.

Preferred Qualifications
- Familiarity with deep learning frameworks such as TensorFlow or PyTorch is a plus.
- Exposure to NLP would be an advantage.
- Experience with Power BI or Tableau is desirable.
- Awareness of cloud platforms such as AWS is a bonus.

Eligibility
- Pursuing a Bachelor's or Master's in Computer Science, Statistics or related.
